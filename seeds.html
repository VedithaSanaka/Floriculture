Seed Quality Testing

Visual Inspection

Check for uniform size, shape, and color. Avoid seeds with cracks, mold, or other imperfections.

Float Test

Place seeds in a bowl of water. Viable seeds will sink, while non-viable seeds will float. Let the seeds sit for about 15-30 minutes.

Germination Test

Use moist paper towels, a plastic bag, and a warm location to test seed viability:

  • Dampen a paper towel and place a few seeds on it.
  • Fold the paper towel over the seeds and place it in a plastic bag.
  • Keep the bag in a warm spot, such as on top of a refrigerator.
  • Check the seeds after a few days to see if they have sprouted.

Cut Test

Carefully cut a few seeds open and examine the insides. A healthy seed will have a firm, white interior.

Additional Notes

For best results, use a sufficient number of seeds (at least 10-20) for testing to get reliable results. Be patient, as some seeds may take longer to show signs of germination.
